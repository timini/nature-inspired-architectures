# References

All biological and conceptual references used in the catalogue and its initial research discussion are retained here. Additional molecular proofreading and synthetic morphogenesis papers are labelled as further reading. This is a curated starting bibliography, not a systematic or exhaustive literature review.

Citation keys are stable across the [BibTeX file](references.bib), [machine-readable metadata](metadata.json), and pattern pages. Bibliographic details were checked against linked primary or institutional records on 2026-10-13; linked abstracts and accessible text were used, and full-text access is not claimed for every source. Papers are linked, not redistributed. Software translations and all diagrams are original interpretations.

<a id="simon1962"></a>
## simon1962

Simon, Herbert A. (1962). **The Architecture of Complexity.** *Proceedings of the American Philosophical Society*.

[Source](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/15-440/assets/READINGS/simon-architecture-of-complexity-1962.pdf)

**Evidence:** Conceptual theory.

Hierarchy, stable intermediate structures and near-decomposability; foundational framing, not an empirical universal law.

<a id="hartwell1999"></a>
## hartwell1999

Hartwell, Leland H.; Hopfield, John J.; Leibler, Stanislas; Murray, Andrew W. (1999). **From molecular to modular cell biology.** *Nature*.

[Source](https://murraylab.fas.harvard.edu/publications/molecular-modular-cell-biology) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/35011540)

**Evidence:** Conceptual synthesis.

Functional molecular modules; does not establish that cells have perfectly isolated software-like interfaces.

<a id="alon2007"></a>
## alon2007

Alon, Uri (2007). **Network motifs: theory and experimental approaches.** *Nature Reviews Genetics*.

[Source](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2102) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg2102)

**Evidence:** Review of theory and experiments.

Regulatory motifs and their dynamical functions. Persistence detection requires the appropriate regulatory logic and kinetics.

<a id="csete2004"></a>
## csete2004

Csete, Marie; Doyle, John (2004). **Bow ties, metabolism and disease.** *Trends in Biotechnology*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15331224/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tibtech.2004.07.007)

**Evidence:** Architectural synthesis.

Shared cores connect diverse inputs and outputs, with robustness and fragility trade-offs.

<a id="gerhart2007"></a>
## gerhart2007

Gerhart, John; Kirschner, Marc (2007). **The theory of facilitated variation.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1876433/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0701035104)

**Evidence:** Evolutionary theory and synthesis.

Conserved core processes, flexible regulation, compartmentation and exploratory behaviour. Recipe recomposition illustrates only a small part of this theory.

<a id="prabhakar2012"></a>
## prabhakar2012

Prabhakar, Balaji; Dektar, Katherine N.; Gordon, Deborah M. (2012). **The Regulation of Ant Colony Foraging Activity without Spatial Information.** *PLOS Computational Biology*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3426560/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1002670)

**Evidence:** Field experiments and computational model.

Local encounters regulate harvester-ant departures. These ants do not need pheromone trails to locate their scattered seeds.

<a id="stouffer2011"></a>
## stouffer2011

Stouffer, Daniel B.; Bascompte, Jordi (2011). **Compartmentalization increases food-web persistence.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3048152/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1014353108)

**Evidence:** Ecological network modelling.

Evidence for compartment effects within the studied food-web models, not a universal guarantee of ecosystem or software stability.

<a id="west2015"></a>
## west2015

West, Stuart A.; Fisher, Roberta M.; Gardner, Andy; Kiers, E. Toby (2015). **Major evolutionary transitions in individuality.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4547252/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1421402112)

**Evidence:** Evolutionary synthesis.

Cooperation, specialisation and conflict reduction in transitions to higher-level individuals; background rather than an implemented algorithm.

<a id="kashtan2005"></a>
## kashtan2005

Kashtan, Nadav; Alon, Uri (2005). **Spontaneous evolution of modularity and network motifs.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16174729/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0503610102)

**Evidence:** Computational evolution experiments.

Modularly varying goals can favour modular networks in the studied models. No evolutionary search is implemented in this repository.

<a id="edelman2001"></a>
## edelman2001

Edelman, Gerald M.; Gally, Joseph A. (2001). **Degeneracy and complexity in biological systems.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.231499798) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.231499798)

**Evidence:** Conceptual synthesis with biological examples.

Structurally different elements can share functions while retaining distinct capabilities. Motivates diverse fallbacks.

<a id="csete2002"></a>
## csete2002

Csete, Marie E.; Doyle, John C. (2002). **Reverse engineering of biological complexity.** *Science*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11872830/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1069981)

**Evidence:** Biology-engineering synthesis.

Connects modularity, protocol hierarchies, feedback, robustness and cascading fragility.

<a id="abelson2000"></a>
## abelson2000

Abelson, Harold; Allen, Don; Coore, Daniel; Hanson, Chris; Homsy, George; Knight, Thomas F.; Nagpal, Radhika; Rauch, Erik; Sussman, Gerald Jay; Weiss, Ron (2000). **Amorphous computing.** *Communications of the ACM*.

[Source](https://groups.csail.mit.edu/mac/projects/amorphous/cacm-2000.html) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/332833.332842)

**Evidence:** Computing research programme.

Biologically inspired coordination of many unreliable, locally connected computational elements.

<a id="kitano2004"></a>
## kitano2004

Kitano, Hiroaki (2004). **Biological robustness.** *Nature Reviews Genetics*.

[Source](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrg1471) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrg1471)

**Evidence:** Review.

Robustness through control, alternative mechanisms, modularity and decoupling; useful background across the catalogue.

<a id="gordon2016"></a>
## gordon2016

Gordon, Deborah M. (2016). **The Evolution of the Algorithms for Collective Behavior.** *Cell Systems*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28009263/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cels.2016.10.013)

**Evidence:** Conceptual synthesis.

Local interaction rules depend on environmental conditions, resource distribution and operating costs.

<a id="yi2000"></a>
## yi2000

Yi, Tau-Mu; Huang, Yun; Simon, Melvin I.; Doyle, John (2000). **Robust perfect adaptation in bacterial chemotaxis through integral feedback control.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC18287/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.97.9.4649)

**Evidence:** Control-theoretic analysis of a biological mechanism.

Integral feedback explains robust adaptation under specified conditions. Our service controller is not a biochemical simulation.

<a id="waters2005"></a>
## waters2005

Waters, Christopher M.; Bassler, Bonnie L. (2005). **Quorum sensing: cell-to-cell communication in bacteria.** *Annual Review of Cell and Developmental Biology*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16212498/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.cellbio.21.012704.131001)

**Evidence:** Review.

Chemical communication and collective regulation. Signal concentration also depends on transport and loss, not just cell count.

<a id="hopfield1974"></a>
## hopfield1974

Hopfield, John J. (1974). **Kinetic Proofreading: A New Mechanism for Reducing Errors in Biosynthetic Processes Requiring High Specificity.** *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences*.

[Source](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC434344/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.71.10.4135)

**Evidence:** Mechanistic theory; further reading.

Candidate for a future pattern on repeated discrimination with energy and time costs. Ordinary repeated validation is not equivalent to kinetic proofreading.

<a id="toda2018"></a>
## toda2018

Toda, Satoshi; Blauch, Lucas R.; Tang, Sindy K. Y.; Morsut, Leonardo; Lim, Wendell A. (2018). **Programming self-organizing multicellular structures with synthetic cell-cell signaling.** *Science*.

[Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29853554/) · [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat0271)

**Evidence:** Synthetic-biology experiment; further reading.

Engineered cell signalling and adhesion yield self-organising structures. These are engineered biological systems, not direct evidence for their evolutionary origin.
